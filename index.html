Help Center

How do I create a request?

Open Growth Center, enter your username, choose a service and quantity, then submit your feedback.

What services can I preview?

Followers, Likes and Views are available as local preview selections.

Does this change my TikTok account?

No. This application creates local previews only. It does not add followers, likes or views.

Where is my request history stored?

History is stored locally in your browser on this device.

What information goes to Telegram?

Only the username and feedback you submit are sent to the website administrator.
